HOW TO ADD YOUR CAR PHOTOGRAPHS:
1. Place your vehicle photos in this folder: public/car-images/
   Example: innova-crysta.jpg, ertiga.jpg, dzire.jpg

2. In src/data/vehicles.ts:
   - Set hasActualPhoto: true
   - Set imageSrc: '/car-images/innova-crysta.jpg'

The website will immediately display your actual car photograph instead of the placeholder!
